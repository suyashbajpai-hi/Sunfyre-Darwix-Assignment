# Sentinel Health - Underwriting & Qualification Rules (Internal)

Document owner: Underwriting | Version 3.2 | Effective 1 April 2024 | Supersedes v3.1 (Oct-2023)

## 1. Entry age

- Essential and Family Shield: adults aged 18 to 65 years at policy start. Dependent children: 91 days to 25 years.
- Senior Care: 61 to 80 years at entry.
- Note (legacy text retained from v2.0): "Essential plan entry age is 18 to 60 years." -- THIS LINE IS OUTDATED, see rule above.

## 2. Medical underwriting triggers

A pre-policy medical examination (PPME) is required when ANY of the following is true:

1. Proposer or any insured adult is above 45 yrs of age.
2. Any pre-existing disease is declared.
3. BMI is below 17 or above 32.
4. Sum insured requested is Rs 25 lakh or more.
5. Plan is Senior Care (always).

PPME is arranged at a Sentinel-empanelled diagnostic centre at Sentinel's cost. If the proposal is not accepted, Sentinel still bears the test cost.

## 3. Pre-existing disease (PED) acceptance grid

| Condition | Decision | Loading | PED waiting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hypertension, controlled on <=2 drugs | Accept | 10% | 36 months (12 months Senior Care) |
| Type 2 diabetes, HbA1c < 8, no complications | Accept | 10-25% by age | 36 months (12 months Senior Care) |
| Type 2 diabetes with complications, or Type 1 / insulin-dependent | Refer to underwriter | case basis | - |
| Asthma, mild, no hospitalisation in 2 yrs | Accept | 0-10% | 36 months |
| Thyroid disorder on medication | Accept | 0% | 36 months |
| Cancer, any history within 5 yrs | Decline | - | - |
| Heart attack / bypass / angioplasty | Decline (Essential, Family Shield); Refer (Senior Care) | - | - |
| Chronic kidney disease stage 3+ | Decline | - | - |
| Obesity BMI > 35 | Refer | 25%+ | - |
| Tobacco use (any form) | Accept | 15% | - |

## 4. Occupation

Hazardous occupations (mining, offshore, armed forces combat roles, stunt work) are referred to the underwriter. All other occupations are accepted at standard rates.

## 5. Geography

Residents of all Indian states and union territories are eligible. Premium zone: Zone A (Mumbai MMR, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad) pays base rate; Zone B (all other cities) gets a 10% discount on base rate. Treatment anywhere in India is covered regardless of zone.

## 6. Lead qualification criteria (for tele-sales and voice agents)

A lead is **Qualified** when all of the following are captured and acceptable:

- Age of oldest proposed member is within the plan's entry range.
- Number of members and relationship (self / spouse / children / parents).
- City or PIN code (to determine zone).
- Declared pre-existing conditions do not fall in the Decline list.
- Desired sum insured is one of the available options.
- Customer agrees to a follow-up (quotation or callback).

A lead is **Referred** when a PED requires underwriter review, when a hazardous occupation is declared, or when details are conflicting (for example a stated age that does not match the stated date of birth). Conflicts must be clarified with the customer; if they cannot be resolved on the call, mark the lead as Referred and do NOT promise eligibility.

A lead is **Not Eligible** when age is outside all plan ranges or a declared condition is in the Decline list. Agents must communicate this politely and offer to share information on the Senior Care plan where age-appropriate.

Agents must never state that a customer is "approved" or "guaranteed" cover. Eligibility is always preliminary and subject to underwriting.

## 7. Premium indication

Indicative premiums may be quoted from the approved rate card (plan_rates.csv). Any quote must be stated as approximate and exclusive of 18% GST. Final premium is confirmed in the quotation document.
