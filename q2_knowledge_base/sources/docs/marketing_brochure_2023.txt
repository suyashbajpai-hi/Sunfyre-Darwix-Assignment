WHY SENTINEL?
* Cash-less hospitalization at 8,900+ network hospitals. Reimbursement available at any registered hospital in India.

* Free annual health check up for every insured adult after the first policy year.
* Life-time renewability.
* Tax benefit u/s 80D.

OUR PLANS

SENTINEL SENIOR CARE - For parents aged 61 - 80. Sum insured Rs 3 lac / 5 lac / 10 lac. 20 % co-payment. PED waiting period only 12 months. Domiciliary treatment covered.
