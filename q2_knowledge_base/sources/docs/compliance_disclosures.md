# Mandatory Call Disclosures & Conduct Rules (Compliance)

Compliance Dept. | Version 1.4 | Dated 2024-03-01 | Applies to all outbound and inbound tele-sales calls, including AI voice agents.

## Mandatory disclosures

1. **Identity & purpose** (start of call): State the agent's name, that the call is from Sentinel Health Insurance, and the purpose of the call.
2. **Call recording consent** (start of call): "This call is being recorded for quality and training purposes." Proceed only if the customer does not object.
3. **Solicitation statement** (before any product discussion): "Insurance is the subject matter of solicitation."
4. **Waiting period disclosure** (before discussing or quoting a plan): State the 30-day initial waiting period, the pre-existing disease waiting period applicable to the plan, and the 24-month specific-illness waiting period.
5. **Premium disclaimer** (whenever a premium figure is mentioned): Premium is indicative, exclusive of 18% GST, and subject to underwriting.
6. **Eligibility disclaimer** (whenever eligibility is discussed): Eligibility is preliminary and subject to underwriting; no guarantee of acceptance.
7. **Free-look period** (when the customer agrees to proceed): 30-day free-look from receipt of policy document.

## Prohibited statements (risky statements)

Agents and voice bots must NOT:

- say or imply cover is "guaranteed", "approved", "100% covered", or that there are "no exclusions";
- promise claim settlement, a settlement timeline shorter than the documented SLA, or "no questions asked" claims;
- say a pre-existing disease will be covered "from day one";
- state premium figures without the GST and underwriting disclaimer;
- give medical advice or tell the customer not to disclose a condition;
- compare competitors negatively or quote competitor prices;
- offer discounts, gifts or cashback that are not on the approved rate card (rebating is prohibited under Section 41 of the Insurance Act).

## Data handling

- Collect only the data required for qualification: name, age or date of birth, city/PIN, number and age of members, declared health conditions, contact number, preferred callback time.
- Do not ask for or record card numbers, bank passwords, OTPs, or Aadhaar numbers on a sales call.
- Transcripts stored for training must have PII masked.

## Do-not-call

If the customer asks not to be contacted again, acknowledge, stop the pitch, and mark the lead DNC. Do not attempt to retain.
