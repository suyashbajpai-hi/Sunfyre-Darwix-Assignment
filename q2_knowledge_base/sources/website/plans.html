Our health insurance plans

All plans cover in-patient hospitalisation (minimum 24 hours), day-care procedures, pre-hospitalisation expenses for 60 days and post-hospitalisation expenses for 90 days, road ambulance up to Rs 3,000 per hospitalisation, and AYUSH treatment at government-recognised hospitals.

Sentinel Essential

An affordable individual plan for young adults and first-time buyers. Sum insured options of Rs 3 lakh, 5 lakh and 7.5 lakh. Room rent capped at 1% of sum insured per day (ICU 2%). Co-pay of 10% applies on every claim. No restoration benefit. Entry age 18 to 65 years.

Sentinel Family Shield

A family floater covering self, spouse and up to 4 dependent children (age 91 days to 25 years) under one sum insured. Options of Rs 5 lakh, 10 lakh, 15 lakh and 25 lakh. No room rent cap (any single private AC room). Zero co-pay for members below 61 years at entry. 100% restoration of sum insured once per year for unrelated illnesses. Maternity cover of Rs 50,000 per delivery after a 24-month waiting period (maximum 2 deliveries). Entry age 18 to 65 years for adults.

Sentinel Senior Care

Designed for parents and senior citizens aged 61 to 80 at entry. Sum insured options of Rs 3 lakh, 5 lakh and 10 lakh. Mandatory co-payment of 20% on all claims. Pre-existing disease waiting period reduced to 12 months. Includes home-care (domiciliary) treatment up to 10% of sum insured and 100% restoration benefit. Pre-policy medical check-up is compulsory.

Optional riders (add-ons)

  • Critical Illness Rider - lump sum payout of Rs 5 lakh to Rs 25 lakh on first diagnosis of any of 20 listed critical illnesses (cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, etc.). 90-day waiting period and 30-day survival period apply.
  • Hospital Cash Rider - Rs 1,000 to Rs 3,000 per day of hospitalisation, from day 2, maximum 30 days a year.
  • Co-pay Waiver - removes the 20% co-payment on Senior Care for an additional premium of roughly 18%.
  • OPD Cover - doctor consultations, diagnostics and pharmacy up to Rs 10,000 or Rs 20,000 a year.

Premiums are exclusive of GST at 18%. Premiums are subject to change with IRDAI approval. Rates shown on this page were last updated on 01/04/2024.
