Frequently asked questions

Buying a policy

Who can buy a Sentinel health policy?

Any Indian resident between 18 and 65 years of age can buy Essential or Family Shield. Senior Care is for people aged 61 to 80. Children from 91 days to 25 years can be covered as dependents under Family Shield. NRIs can buy a policy but treatment is covered only within India.

Do I need a medical test before buying?

If you are 45 or younger and have no pre-existing disease, no medical test is required - you can buy on the basis of your health declaration. If you are above 45, or you declare any pre-existing condition such as diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorder or asthma, we may ask for a pre-policy check-up. Sentinel pays for the test. Senior Care always requires a check-up.

What is a pre-existing disease (PED)?

A pre-existing disease is any condition, ailment or injury that was diagnosed, or for which medical advice or treatment was received, within 48 months before your first policy with us. PEDs are covered after the PED waiting period - 36 months on Essential and Family Shield, 12 months on Senior Care.

I have diabetes. Can I still buy?

Yes, in most cases. Controlled Type 2 diabetes (HbA1c below 8) is accepted with a 36-month PED waiting period and, depending on age, a premium loading of 10% to 25%. Insulin-dependent diabetes or diabetes with complications (kidney, eye or nerve damage) is referred to our underwriting team and may be declined.

Can I get cover if I smoke?

Yes. Smokers and tobacco users pay a loading of 15% on the base premium. You must declare tobacco use on the proposal form; non-disclosure can lead to claim rejection.

Premiums and payment

How much does it cost?

Premium depends on plan, age of the oldest member, sum insured, city tier and any loadings. For example, a 30-year-old buying Sentinel Essential with Rs 5 lakh sum insured pays about Rs 7,200 a year plus GST. A family of two adults aged 35 and 33 with one child buying Family Shield Rs 10 lakh pays about Rs 18,900 a year plus GST. Ask our advisor for an exact quote.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Annual, half-yearly, quarterly and monthly payment frequencies are available. Monthly payment carries a 4% frequency loading and requires auto-debit (NACH or card standing instruction).

What happens if I miss a premium payment?

You get a grace period of 30 days (15 days for monthly mode) to pay without losing continuity benefits. If you do not pay within the grace period, the policy lapses and any claim arising during the lapse period is not payable. Waiting periods restart if the lapse exceeds 30 days.

Will my premium increase every year?

Premium changes only when you move into a higher age band (every 5 years) or when IRDAI approves a revision of the product's rates. We do not increase an individual's premium because they made a claim.

Claims

How do cashless claims work?

Show your Sentinel health card at any network hospital. The hospital sends a pre-authorisation request; we usually approve within 2 hours for planned admissions and within 60 minutes for emergencies. At discharge we settle the bill directly with the hospital. You pay only non-covered items and any applicable copayment.

How do I claim at a non-network hospital?

Pay the hospital, then submit the discharge summary, itemised bills, payment receipts, prescriptions and investigation reports through the Sentinel app or at a branch within 30 days of discharge. Reimbursement is credited to your bank account within 7 working days of receiving complete documents.

Why do claims get rejected?

The main reasons are: non-disclosure of a pre-existing disease at the time of buying, claims during a waiting period, treatment that is excluded (for example cosmetic surgery), hospitalisation that was not medically necessary, and incomplete documents. Sentinel's rejection rate is under 4%.

Policy servicing

Can I port my existing policy to Sentinel?

Yes. Under IRDAI portability rules you can move to Sentinel at renewal time and carry forward the waiting periods you have already served with your previous insurer. Apply at least 45 days before your current policy expires.

Can I cancel after buying?

You have a free-look period of 30 days from receiving the policy document. If you cancel within the free look, we refund the premium minus the cost of any medical tests and pro-rata risk premium for the days covered.

Is there a co-pay?

Essential has a 10% copayment on every claim. Family Shield has no copayment unless a member was 61 or older at entry, in which case 20% applies to that member. Senior Care has a 20% copayment, which can be removed with the Co-pay Waiver rider.

What is not covered?

Common exclusions: cosmetic or plastic surgery (unless after an accident), dental treatment unless caused by accident, infertility and IVF, treatment for alcohol or drug abuse, self-inflicted injuries, war and nuclear risks, experimental treatments, and expenses outside India.
