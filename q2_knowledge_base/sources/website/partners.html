Become a Sentinel branch partner

Sentinel works with 1,100 partner outlets - banks, NBFCs, pharmacies and independent agencies - who distribute our health plans in their communities.

Branch partnership benefits

Operational, marketing, and technology support is provided to branch partners. This includes a co-branded storefront kit, a tablet-based proposal app that issues policies in under 10 minutes, a dedicated relationship manager, and a shared marketing fund of up to Rs 1 lakh per outlet per year.

Commission structure

Partners earn a first-year commission of 15% on Essential and Family Shield and 10% on Senior Care, with renewal commission of 7.5% from the second year onwards. Commission is paid monthly by the 10th of the following month.

Eligibility

Partners must hold a valid IRDAI corporate agent or POSP licence, have a physical outlet, and complete Sentinel's 2-day product certification. A minimum of 20 policies per quarter is expected after the first six months.
