About Sentinel Health Insurance

Sentinel Health Insurance Company Ltd. is a standalone health insurer licensed by the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), registration no. 162. Our head office is in Pune, Maharashtra and we operate 84 branches and 1,100 partner outlets nationwide.

Our claim settlement ratio for FY 2023-24 was 96.4%. Claims are processed in-house; we do not use a third-party administrator (TPA), which is why our cashless pre-authorisation is typically approved within 2 hours.

Why choose Sentinel?

  • Cashless hospitalisation at 9,500+ network hospitals. Reimbursement is available at any registered hospital in India.
  • Restoration benefit: if you exhaust your sum insured during the policy year, we restore 100% of it once for unrelated illnesses (Family Shield and Senior Care plans).
  • No-claim bonus: sum insured increases by 10% for every claim-free year, up to a maximum of 50%.
  • Free annual health check-up for every insured adult after the first policy year.
  • Lifetime renewability - once you are a member, we never refuse renewal on grounds of age or claims history.
  • Tax benefit under Section 80D of the Income Tax Act for premiums paid (as per prevailing tax laws).

How to buy

  1. Talk to a Sentinel advisor or request a call back.
  2. Complete the proposal form and tell us about your medical history honestly. Non-disclosure is the number one reason claims are rejected across the industry.
  3. Members above 45 years, or anyone declaring a pre-existing condition, may be asked for a medical check-up at a Sentinel-paid diagnostic centre.
  4. Pay the first premium. Your policy document is emailed within 24 hours and the 30-day initial waiting period begins from the policy start date.
