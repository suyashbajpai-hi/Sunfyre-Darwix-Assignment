# ShieldLife × Banco Isla — Product Guide (Philippines)

Bancassurance desk | Version 2.1 | Effective 2024-06-01 | Market: PH | Language: Filipino / English / Taglish

This is **not** a health-insurance document. ShieldLife is a life insurer distributing through Banco Isla branches.

## ShieldLife Term Protect

Term life with level premium. Coverage 5, 10, 15 or 20 years, or up to age 65. Sum insured ₱500,000 to ₱10,000,000. Medical exam required above ₱3,000,000 or age 50. No cash value. If the policy **lapses** after the 31-day grace period, coverage stops; reinstatement needs new underwriting within 2 years.

Premium example (non-smoker): 35-year-old male, ₱1,000,000, 20-year term ≈ ₱1,150 / month. Indicative only, exclusive of documentary stamp tax.

## ShieldLife Whole Life

Whole-life participating plan. Premiums payable to age 60 or 20 years, whichever later. Builds cash value after year 3. Optional **riders**: accidental death, waiver of premium, critical illness (20 conditions, 90-day waiting, 30-day survival). Beneficiary must be named; if none, estate.

"Magkano ang premium" depends on age, gender, smoker status, sum insured — never quote a number that is not on the rate card.

## Credit Life (bank referral)

Credit life covers the outstanding Banco Isla personal loan or housing loan if the borrower dies. Distributed **only** as a bank referral from a Banco Isla relationship manager. Sum insured = outstanding principal, declining. Premium is added to the loan amortization, not billed separately.

Do not offer credit life to someone with no Banco Isla loan. Do not say the loan is "automatically approved" if they buy life insurance.
