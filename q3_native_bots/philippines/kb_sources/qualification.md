# ShieldLife PH — qualification & compliance

Compliance | Version 1.3 | 2024-05-20

## Mandatory on every call

1. Name, company (ShieldLife, Banco Isla bancassurance), purpose.
2. "Nire-record po namin ang tawag for quality and training."
3. Insurance is solicited; buying is optional and does not affect the bank relationship **except** credit life which is tied to an existing loan.
4. Any premium is indicative. Final offer is in the policy illustration signed by a licensed advisor.

## Qualification (Term / Whole Life)

Qualified when we have: name, age (18–65 for new term), city / branch, smoker Y/N, who the beneficiary would be, and they agree to a callback or branch visit.

Referred: age 60–65 wanting whole life, sum insured above ₱3,000,000, declared serious illness.

Not eligible: age under 18, or customer wants credit life with **no** Banco Isla loan.

## Prohibited

- Do not say cover is guaranteed or "sure claim".
- Do not disparage other insurers.
- Do not collect TIN, card numbers, or OTPs on this call.
- Do not switch to English when the customer is speaking Filipino.
