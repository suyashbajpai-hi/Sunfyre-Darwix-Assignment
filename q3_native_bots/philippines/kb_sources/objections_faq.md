# ShieldLife — Objection & FAQ playbook (PH)

Sales enablement | 2024.2 | Taglish approved lines. Do **not** literally translate the India health-insurance playbook.

## Objection: "Mahal ang premium / I cannot afford it."

Approved: "Gets ko po. For Term Protect, a 35-year-old looking at ₱1M coverage is around ₱1,150 a month — less than a streaming bundle. We can step the sum insured down to ₱500,000 if that is kinder on the budget. Credit life, if you have a Banco Isla loan, is built into the amortization so there is no extra hulog sa isip."

Do not: invent a discount or waive underwriting.

## Objection: "May insurance na ako sa office."

Approved: "Maganda po iyon. Group cover from HR usually ends when you resign, and the beneficiary is often the estate, not the asawa. Personal term life stays with you and you name the beneficiary. Many clients keep both."

## Objection: "Baka mag-lapse, sayang."

Approved: "May 31-day grace period po. We can set autopay from your Banco Isla payroll account so the premium does not slip. If it still lapses, reinstatement is possible within 2 years with new health questions — hindi automatic."

## Objection: "I will talk to my asawa first."

Approved: "Siyempre po. I can send a one-pager on WhatsApp and set a callback when you are both free — gabi or weekend is fine."

## FAQ: Sino ang pwedeng maging beneficiary?

Spouse, children, parents, or any person with insurable interest. For credit life the **bank is the primary beneficiary** up to the outstanding loan; any excess goes to the family beneficiary.

## FAQ: Pwede ba ang smoker?

Yes, with smoker rates (roughly 1.6× non-smoker on Term Protect). Non-disclosure of tobacco is a claim issue.

## Escalation

Transfer to a licensed advisor / branch when the customer asks for a tao, a manager, a policy illustration PDF with signed figures, a complaint, or a product not in this knowledge base. Stay in their language — do not bounce into English.
